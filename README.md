Saturday 21 November 2026: 37 dated stories, 6 regions, one date check. One page summing up the day two tankers were hit off Oman while a plant burned in Bahrain, COP31 ran into overtime in Antalya, a deep 6.7 earthquake shook Peru, Bitcoin printed a two-year low, and BepiColombo reached Mercury — three large illustrated scenes, ten more illustrated story cards, a world map with numbered pins, a six-number stat bar and a region tally, 24 further briefs and results, every story verified against wire reporting and date-checked on the page itself (the DATE CHECK band names the famous stories that were *not* Saturday's and were left out).

The image is drawn by `draw.py` (a build tool, Pillow) and committed as `entry.png`; `main.py` is a stdlib-only copier that verifies and copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
